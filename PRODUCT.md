# TrackRank

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Agency analysts monitoring client brands and in-house marketers monitoring their own brands have equal priority. Both need to explain where a brand appears in AI answers and show the evidence behind a finding. Agency analysts also need to move between clients without losing track of whose data they are viewing.

## Product Purpose

Help a marketer answer three questions. Which customer questions did we track? Where did our brand appear? Which recorded answers and sources support that conclusion?

## Positioning

TrackRank connects a question, engine, location and collection time to a recorded answer and its citations. Teams supply their own collection credentials. They can use the hosted service or self-host the open-source application.

## Operating Context

An organization owns team access, credentials and billing. Workspaces group brands and questions for a client, brand or campaign. A team can paste a batch of questions, review engine variants, collect once or schedule future checks, then share a link to a finding or export run history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Show the distinction between saving questions, saving a paused schedule and starting a collection that can incur provider charges.
- Keep organic search results separate from AI answer citations. Distinguish missing answers and failed runs from successful observations.
- Keep historical evidence accessible when a team archives a prompt. Keep the scope of a finding identifiable when following a shared link.
- Explain the hosted allowance, searchable history and separate provider costs. The hosted trial lasts 14 days, requires no card and does not automatically charge the team.
- Support bulk workflows, keyboard operation and equivalent REST and MCP actions.

## Evidence on Hand

Stored runs can supply answer text, citations, grounding queries, timestamps and brand observations. Use permissioned, appropriately labeled examples for public demonstrations. The owner describes TrackRank as prelaunch with no users yet.

## Product Principles

1. Let readers trace a reported number to the observations behind it.
2. Make client and workspace context clear before a user acts or shares a finding.
3. Let a team prepare monitoring without spending money. Make the action that starts collection explicit.
4. Design the first successful investigation for both primary audiences. Treat the current visual guide as provisional.
